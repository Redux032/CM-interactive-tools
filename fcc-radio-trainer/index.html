How to Get Your Ham Radio Operator Certificate

Step by step, from studying to call sign
Step 1 — Get your FCC Registration Number (FRN) do this BEFORE exam day
You cannot register for an exam session without an FRN. It's free and takes about 10 minutes.
  • Go to FCC CORES and click Register (under "Need a Username?") to create an FCC Username account using a valid email.
  • Check your email and click the verification link to activate the account.
  • Log back in to CORES, choose Register New FRN, select Individual, and fill in your details (this step asks for your SSN, handled securely by the FCC).
  • Submit — your FRN appears immediately on the confirmation page. Write it down; you'll use it to register for your exam and it stays with you for life.
Step 2 — Find an exam session (remote or in person)
  • Open hamstudy.org/sessions/remote. It lists online/remote sessions you can take from home over Zoom; drop the "/remote" for in-person sessions near you.
  • Pick a session and click the orange Register button.
  • You'll get a confirmation email with a link to pay the exam fee (typically ~$15 to the volunteer examiner team) and choose your time slot.
  • For a remote exam you'll need a reliable internet connection, a webcam, a quiet private room, and two devices (e.g., a computer plus a phone) for the Zoom proctoring.
Step 3 — Exam day & after you pass
  • Bring your FRN and a photo ID. The Technician exam is 35 questions; you need 26 correct.
  • After passing, there's a $35 FCC application fee for the new license. Your call sign is typically issued within a few business days and appears in the FCC ULS database.
  • Once your call sign is granted, you're on the air. Congratulations, and 73!
